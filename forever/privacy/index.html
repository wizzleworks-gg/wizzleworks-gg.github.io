Forever Talent Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Forever has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It doesn't collect personal data.

Your builds

Builds you save stay on your device. They're included in your device's own backups if you have backups turned on, and nowhere else.

Talent data

To keep up with the game, the app downloads talent data and icons from data.wizzleworks.gg, which is served through Cloudflare. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the app's name and iOS version. Our server keeps a log of these requests for 14 days to keep the service running; the log records Cloudflare's address, not yours. Cloudflare handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Update check

The app asks the App Store for the latest version of Forever so it can tell you when an update is available. Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens an email to wizzleworks@icloud.com in Mail, filled in with the app version, your device model and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you send it. Emails are used only to reply and to improve the app.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: wizzleworks@icloud.com.
